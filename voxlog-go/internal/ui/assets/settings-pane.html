While this is on, macOS keeps its microphone indicator lit -- the machine really is listening. Voxlog holds about a minute of sound in memory and writes nothing to disk until it is sure somebody is speaking; a recording that turns out to be one voice is filed in History as a note, and one with a second voice in it becomes a meeting.
